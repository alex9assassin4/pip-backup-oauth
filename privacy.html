Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Purpose

Pip Backup is a personal, single-user backup utility. It uses Google OAuth solely to let its owner copy and verify files between their local computer and their own Google Drive account.

Data handling

File data travels directly between the owner's computer and Google Drive. Pip Backup has no developer-operated server, user database, analytics, advertising, or telemetry. It does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not transmitted to this website or published in its source code.

Retention and control

Backed-up files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deliberately removes them. Google access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.

Scope

This application and policy are for the owner's private use only. The website collects no personal information.
